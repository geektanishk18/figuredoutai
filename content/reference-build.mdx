## Architecture

Four connected surfaces, built as one system rather than assembled from parts:

- **Buyer-facing portal** — the public surface where an enquiry begins, with listing context carried
  into the conversation rather than discarded at the form
- **WhatsApp qualification layer** — first response and qualifying questions on the channel buyers
  in this market actually use, capturing structured answers instead of a transcript
- **Admin panel** — where the operator reads what the layer decided, on which rule, and overrides
  anything it got wrong
- **Voice qualification** — the same rule set applied to inbound calls, so an enquiry is qualified
  identically whichever way it arrives

The point of building all four is that a decision layer is only as good as its coverage. A rule set
that applies on the website and not on the phone produces exactly the inconsistency it was meant to
remove.

## The qualification logic

Property enquiries look uniform and are not. The logic separated them on the dimensions that
actually predict whether a site visit happens:

- **Intent** — buying to live in it, or as an investment
- **Timeline** — possession needed within months, or browsing for next year
- **Budget band** — checked against what is genuinely available in that region
- **Region and project** — which inventory the enquiry maps onto
- **Channel and source** — where the enquiry came from, carried through to routing

From those, the layer scored the enquiry, routed it to the regional team that owned that inventory,
and escalated with the answers already attached. A site visit was booked inside the conversation
rather than after a callback.

## Screens

The admin panel was built around the decision log rather than around a message inbox. The operator's
first view is what arrived, what the system decided, and why — with the conversation available
underneath rather than in front.

Override tracking sits alongside it. When a human disagreed with a routing decision, that
disagreement was recorded against the rule that produced it, which is what makes the rule set
improvable rather than merely deployed.
