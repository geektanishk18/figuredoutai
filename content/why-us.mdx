## 01 · The gap is the logic, not the model

Today's models are more than capable of running a qualifying conversation. Almost nothing deployed
in the Indian market actually does.

What gets sold is a model connected to a knowledge base. It answers. It cannot tell an urgent
enquiry from a casual one, apply your criteria, or decide who should own what — so every decision
still waits for a person. We build the decision logic first and connect the model to it second,
which is why our first phase is rule design rather than deployment.

## 02 · Your rules, not our template

What makes an enquiry urgent for a diagnostics chain has nothing in common with what makes an
applicant worth a recruiter's time. We extract your criteria from the people who apply them today,
rather than adjusting a template.

That is where the first uncomfortable discovery usually happens: the criteria exist as instinct in
two or three senior heads, applied inconsistently by everyone below them. Writing them down tends to
change how a business thinks about its own intake, independent of anything built afterwards.

## 03 · Every decision is auditable

Every decision is logged with the rule it applied and the reasoning behind it — what arrived, what
happened, why, and what your team overrode. Override patterns show where your written rules diverge
from your actual judgement, and that gap is where the next improvement comes from.

## 04 · How engagements work

- **The logic belongs to you.** Rules are documented in language your team can read. They are
  configuration, not code, so changing them is an operating task rather than a rebuild.
- **You approve every phase before it is billed.** Every phase is milestone-gated. You approve each
  stage before the next begins. We hold this as a floor condition and do not waive it, because it
  removes the risk that matters most to you: paying for a system that turns out not to fit.
- **Your data stays where it is.** Your data stays in your systems. We document what the layer
  reads, what it stores, and what it never touches.
- **We stay after go-live.** A layer nobody maintains stops matching the business. Our engagements
  are built around the live phase; the build is the shorter part.
- **We tell you when it will not work.** This needs existing enquiry volume and criteria that can be
  defined. If you do not have both, we say so during the audit rather than after the build.
