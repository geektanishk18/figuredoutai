## What the audit actually is

Most "free audits" are a sales call with a nicer name. This one produces a document.

We map how enquiries currently reach your business and where they stop:

1. **Every channel** an enquiry can arrive through — and who owns each one
2. **Response-time reality** — measured against your actual data, not estimated
3. **Where qualification happens today** — and whether it happens consistently or depends on who
   picked up
4. **Which decisions are being made by whoever was available** rather than by whoever should decide
5. **A written intake map** with the specific failure points marked

**Deliverable:** the intake map. Yours to keep, whether or not we work together.

If the volume or the criteria aren't there, we will tell you. That is a legitimate result of an
audit, not a failed sales call.

## What we need from you

Roughly two hours of access across the three days: a conversation with whoever owns intake today,
and read access to your enquiry data. Nothing installed, nothing changed.
