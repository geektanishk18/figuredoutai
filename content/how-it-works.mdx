## Phase 1 — Intake Audit

Three working days. No cost, no obligation.

We map how enquiries currently reach the business and where they stop:

1. Every channel an enquiry can arrive through, and who owns each one
2. Response-time reality — measured, not estimated
3. Where qualification currently happens, and whether it happens consistently
4. Which decisions are being made by whoever happens to be available
5. A written map of the intake path with the specific failure points marked

You keep the written intake map, whether or not we work together.

## Phase 2 — Logic design

We work through your qualification criteria and document the rule set: what counts as urgent, what
disqualifies, what escalates, what routes where, and what happens in every ambiguous case.

This is reviewed and signed off by you before anything is built. It is written in language your team
can read and change, not in code.

## Phase 3 — Build and deploy

We build the layer, integrate it with your existing stack, and run it in parallel with your current
process before it takes live traffic. Nothing is switched over on trust.

## Phase 4 — Managed operation

We stay on. Rules get tuned against real outcomes and the overrides your team actually makes.

A qualification layer that nobody maintains slowly stops matching the business. Our engagements are
built around this phase, not around the build.

## Commercials

One-time setup fee covering discovery, logic design and build, then a phased retainer across the
build and live phases. Infrastructure is billed as pass-through at cost plus margin.

**Milestone-gated payment is a floor condition and is not waived.** You approve each stage before the
next begins and before it is invoiced. If the engagement stops making sense at any gate, you stop
there.

We take on a limited number of engagements concurrently. Understanding an intake path properly takes
attention, and attention does not scale by adding people.
