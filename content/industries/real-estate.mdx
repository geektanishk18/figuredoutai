The broker who replies first is usually the one the buyer talks to.

## How intake fails here

- **Round-robin ignores availability.** A lead assigned to someone driving to a site visit is a lead
  nobody touches for three hours.
- **The same buyer enquires on four listings.** They are handled as four separate leads by four
  people, each starting the conversation from nothing.
- **Intent is invisible.** An end user buying to move in within three months and an investor
  comparing yields need entirely different conversations, and both arrive as the same portal
  notification.
- **Budget mismatch surfaces at the site visit.** An hour of a sales person's time, plus the buyer's
  Saturday, spent discovering something a question could have established.
- **Follow-up decays.** The third day of unanswered messages is when the buyer stops considering you
  and starts considering the next listing.

## What changes

Your sales team opens conversations that already carry intent, budget, timeline and a booked visit.
The first thing they do is talk to a qualified buyer, not establish whether one exists.

Response time stops depending on who happened to be free. Every enquiry gets an immediate, specific
first response, and the ones that deserve a person reach a person with the context attached.
