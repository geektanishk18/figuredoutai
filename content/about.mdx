Most businesses don't lose enquiries because nobody cared. They lose them because the enquiry
arrived at 9:12 on a Tuesday, the person who could act on it was already on a call, and by the time
anyone looked, the buyer had moved on.

That gap between arrival and action is where revenue quietly disappears. It is not a staffing
problem. It is a decision problem — and it is the only thing we work on.

## Why this company exists

Indian businesses have not been under-sold AI. They have been sold the wrong thing: a system that
answers from a script, cannot tell an urgent enquiry from a casual one, and cannot act on the
business's own criteria.

We started FiguredoutAI to build the other kind — the system that decides.

## How we think about it

**Growth in India is still priced in headcount.** More enquiries means more people. More applicants
means more screeners. More locations means more coordinators. Every growth plan eventually becomes a
hiring plan.

We think that link can be broken — not by removing people, but by changing what reaches them.

**The human layer stays. It stops being the first layer.**

## How we work

**We build and deploy.** We are not a strategy firm that hands over a document.

**We stay after go-live.** Qualification rules need tuning as the business changes. A system nobody
maintains reverts to being ignored.

**The logic belongs to you.** Your rules, documented and auditable. Not a black box you rent access
to.

**We take on a limited number of engagements at a time.** This work requires understanding a
business's intake path properly, and that does not parallelise well.
